I want the "Applied"s statuses.
I want those statuses to be in the month of October, 2026.
I want those to be from a specific user, user_id = 2.


I will try implicit join since that makes sense to me:

```sql
SELECT count(S.job_id)
FROM status as S, job as J
WHERE
    S.status_description = 'Applied'
    AND
    S.status_datetime BETWEEN '2026-10-01' AND '2026-10-31'
    AND
    S.job_id = J.job_id
    AND
    J.user_id = 2;
```
- The above sql query worked!
